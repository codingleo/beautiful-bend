# The 100-case coverage matrix

Generated by `python3 scripts/coverage.py` from `tests/cases.json`.
Each row is one distinct entry point and behavioral or rejection contract.
Sample inputs inside a row are assertions, not additional counted cases.

**60 runtime programs + 15 proof projects + 25 expected rejections = 100 cases.**

The count includes the original seven programs (r01–r03, r21, r41–r43),
the original counter proof (p01), and original nine rejection cases (n01–n09).
Thus 83 cases are new. Support modules, 15 fixed-law mutations, two additional proof-guard checks, harness
unit tests and fresh-agent tasks are not counted toward 100.

Runtime outputs are exact assertions established from the stated oracle.
Proof projects require both ordinary checker and no-main proof-gate acceptance;
their 19 law declarations are SHA256-pinned. Every proof project also has a
[targeted mutation](tests/findings/proof-mutations.md) with unchanged LAWS;
13 mutated implementations must still pass standalone checking, while their proofs fail.
The other two mutations reject an incorrect proof/witness. Rejections require exit 1, the Bend failure
verdict, and a case-specific diagnostic. None establishes BendTT, native/GPU
execution, parallel scheduling, performance, or broad agent effectiveness.
